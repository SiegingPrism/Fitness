Phase 1 — Product & System Architecture
We should treat this as the foundation of the entire gym platform. The goal of Phase 1 is to decide exactly what we're building, who uses it, how the pieces interact, and what data the system needs.
Given your earlier coach/athlete dashboard work, I recommend building this as a Coach + Athlete + AI fitness platform rather than a simple gym tracker.

1. Product Vision
Working concept
A fitness platform where coaches manage athletes, athletes execute their programs, and an AI layer continuously analyzes performance and helps personalize training.
The important distinction is:
Most fitness apps:
 
Plan → User follows → Track
 
Our app:
 
Plan
  ↓
Execute
  ↓
Track
  ↓
Analyze
  ↓
AI/Coach evaluates
  ↓
Adapt plan
  ↓
Execute again
So the app becomes a continuous training system, not just a database of workouts.

The training platform is the product. AI is the intelligence layer.

10. Why rir Matters
I'd include RIR — Reps In Reserve from the beginning.
For example:
Bench
60kg × 10
RIR = 2
means the athlete estimates they could have performed roughly two more reps.
This gives the future AI much richer information than simply:
60kg × 10
It can eventually reason about:
Weight
Reps
RIR
Volume
Frequency
Performance trend
rather than blindly increasing weight.

20. One Change I'd Make From the Earlier Architecture
I would not create a separate plannedSets collection.

The actual execution belongs in:

 └── Exercise
      └── Sets
That's a much cleaner MongoDB design.

19. Important MongoDB Rule
We'll use references for large/independent entities and embedding for data that belongs tightly to its parent.
Reference

  ↓
Athlete
 
Workout Session
  ↓
Athlete
Embed

 └── Days
      └── Exercises
           └── Prescription
and:
Workout Session
 └── Exercises
      └── Sets
That's the MongoDB-specific design decision I want to lock in.

Even though Coach A is a coach, they cannot access Athlete 999 unless the relationship exists.
